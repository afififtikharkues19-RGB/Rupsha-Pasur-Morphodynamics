# Verified headline findings

## Central result

The clearest corridor-wide curvature–migration association among the six tested upstream spatial offsets occurred at **2 local channel widths upstream (2B)**.

A +1 SD increase in signed width-normalized curvature (κB) at 2B was associated with **−0.085 m yr⁻¹** of signed mapped-centerline migration (95% CrI **−0.180 to −0.019 m yr⁻¹**; P[β < 0] = **0.993**).

Under the locked downstream-referenced coordinate convention, the negative coefficient corresponds to an **outward-aligned curvature–migration relationship**.

The corridor-wide magnitude is small. The result should therefore be interpreted as a directionally resolved association rather than a large migration effect.

## Spatial consistency

At 2B, **10 of 12** spatial blocks had negative posterior means and 2 had positive posterior means.

No individual block had a 95% credible interval entirely below zero at 2B. Three blocks had P(outward) ≥ 0.90 and one had P(outward) ≥ 0.95.

This supports a **spatially widespread directional tendency with substantial local uncertainty**, not a uniform or universally resolved block-level effect.

## Spatial heterogeneity

Across the complete 0–2.5B spatial-offset domain, four local effects had 95% credible intervals excluding zero:

- **B07**, 63.18–73.62 km: inward-aligned association at **1.5B**, +0.397 m yr⁻¹ per +1 SD κB (95% CrI 0.176 to 0.638).
- **B09**, 84.18–94.50 km: outward-aligned association at **2.5B**, −0.301 m yr⁻¹ (95% CrI −0.601 to −0.034).
- **B10**, 94.62–105.06 km: outward-aligned association at **0.5B**, −0.151 m yr⁻¹ (95% CrI −0.262 to −0.046).
- **B10**, 94.62–105.06 km: outward-aligned association at **1B**, −0.130 m yr⁻¹ (95% CrI −0.237 to −0.025).

The reach-scale results therefore indicate variation in both the **direction** and **spatial offset** of curvature–migration coupling.

## Robustness audit

Post hoc diagnostics were conducted without refitting the frozen production model.

- Predictor linear dependence was weak (maximum Pearson r = 0.296).
- Posterior trade-off among global curvature coefficients was modest (maximum absolute posterior correlation = 0.301).
- The negative 2B descriptive relationship strengthened rather than disappeared when exact-zero extracted displacements were excluded.
- Residual spatial dependence was moderate at immediately adjacent stations but decayed rapidly, approaching zero over approximately 2.4–4.8 km.
- Phase-balanced spatial thinning preserved a negative 2B descriptive relationship through approximately 2.4 km station spacing.
- Posterior predictive checks showed that the continuous Student-t likelihood did not reproduce the large extraction-level exact-zero concentration or the full central response distribution; this remains an explicit model-fit limitation.

## Claim boundary

These results describe conditional associations between antecedent channel geometry and subsequent lateral mapped-centerline migration.

They do **not** establish causation, do **not** demonstrate out-of-sample predictive skill, and do **not** prove that exactly 2B is a universal physical optimum. The mapped margins are satellite-observed outer water–land boundary proxies rather than surveyed geomorphic banks.
