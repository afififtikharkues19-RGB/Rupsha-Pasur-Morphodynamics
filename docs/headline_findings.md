# Verified headline findings

## Central result

The clearest corridor-wide curvature–migration association
occurred at **2.0 local channel widths upstream (2.0B)**.

A +1 SD increase in signed upstream curvature was associated
with **-0.085 m/yr** of signed migration
(95% CrI **-0.180 to -0.019 m/yr**;
P[outward] = **0.993**).

Under the locked downstream-referenced coordinate system,
the negative coefficient corresponds to **outward-aligned
migration**.

## Spatial consistency

At the 2.0B offset, **10 of 12**
spatial blocks had negative posterior means.

This supports a **spatially widespread directional tendency
with substantial local uncertainty**, rather than a signal
confined to only one reach.

## Spatial heterogeneity

Across the complete 0–2.5B lag domain, four local effects had
95% credible intervals excluding zero:

- **B07**, 63.18–73.62 km: inward-aligned response at **1.5B**, 0.397 m/yr per +1 SD curvature (95% CrI 0.176 to 0.638).
- **B09**, 84.18–94.50 km: outward-aligned response at **2.5B**, -0.301 m/yr per +1 SD curvature (95% CrI -0.601 to -0.034).
- **B10**, 94.62–105.06 km: outward-aligned response at **0.5B**, -0.151 m/yr per +1 SD curvature (95% CrI -0.262 to -0.046).
- **B10**, 94.62–105.06 km: outward-aligned response at **1.0B**, -0.130 m/yr per +1 SD curvature (95% CrI -0.237 to -0.025).

The reach-scale results therefore indicate variation in both
the **direction** and **spatial offset** of curvature–migration
coupling.

## Claim boundary

These results describe conditional associations between
antecedent channel geometry and subsequent lateral migration.

They do **not** establish causation and do **not** demonstrate
out-of-sample predictive skill.
