# Project status

This repository documents a research project on Rupsha–Pasur tidal-river morphodynamics.

**Current status: Manuscript in final preparation.**

The primary scientific analysis is complete. The final retained posterior contains 8,000 draws, and the headline inference is based on nominal geometry. Measurement uncertainty is characterized separately using empirical temporal centerline-error distributions and a converged 2,000-realization joint geometry ensemble.

Post hoc robustness diagnostics are also complete. These diagnostics evaluated predictor dependence, posterior coefficient trade-off, exact-zero sensitivity, posterior predictive fit, residual spatial dependence, and phase-balanced spatial thinning without refitting or resampling the production model.

The principal corridor-wide result remains a small but directionally resolved outward-aligned association at the tested 2B upstream spatial offset. Important limitations remain documented, particularly the inability of the continuous Student-t likelihood to reproduce the large extraction-level exact-zero concentration and the presence of localized short-range residual spatial dependence.

Final manuscript assembly, publication-quality figure preparation, and repository documentation are underway. Results should therefore be treated as findings from a manuscript in final preparation rather than as a formally published version of record.
